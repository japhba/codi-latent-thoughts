# Consensus report — blinded systematicity review

Consensus judge over `judge_gemini.md` and `judge_sonnet.md`, both blinded reviews of `packet.json` (28 problems; reader A annotated on 20, reader B on 20, 12 shared — scope counts verified against the packet). Every finding below was spot-checked directly against `packet.json`; spot-check verdicts are marked **[verified]** with the checked example. In addition to per-quote checks, two systematic scans were run over the raw packet: (i) a word-boundary digit scan of all 80 reader-A texts against each problem's own `gold_chain`/`gold_answer` (0 hits), and (ii) the same scan over all 80 reader-B texts (exact gold answer appears in 8/20 problems; per-step counts 2 at step 0 — both incidental/hedged — then 5/6/5 at steps 100/200/300).

## 1. AGREEMENTS

**A1. Reader A's descriptions are never about the actual problem; the register is academic/statistical/consumer-review prose.**
- Gemini: "Reader A exhibits virtually zero topical grounding in the actual grade-school math word problems. Across all checkpoints, it hallucinates contexts such as sports journalism, online bingo guides, casino reviews, and medical regressions."
- Sonnet: "never in the register of a grade-school arithmetic word problem, and never referencing the actual entities of the problem it is nominally describing (no restaurants, cookies, marbles, stickers, turtles, lizards, or lego blocks appear anywhere in A's 80 step-texts)."
- Spot check **[verified]**: g5043 (question: drama-club student counts) A step_0 reads verbatim "The text is a structured FAQ/guide about online bingo, following a consistent pattern of question headers followed by detailed answers with specific examples and statistics." A content-word scan of all 80 A texts against their own questions found zero genuine overlaps (the only string matches were substrings inside unrelated words: "open" in "opens", "mary" in "summary", "pairs" in "pairs findings").

**A2. Reader A recovers no gold values, at any checkpoint.**
- Gemini: "Numerical values in Reader A's text never match the gold intermediate values or gold final answers, instead consisting of generic regression coefficients, p-values, or small decimals."
- Sonnet: "scanning every A text for the gold_chain/gold_answer digits of its own problem ... none appear — A's numbers are all generic exemplars of the statistics genre."
- Spot check **[verified]**: independent digit scan of all 80 A texts against their own gold values: 0 hits. E.g. g5036 (gold 50/75/525) A step_300 expects "a decimal like '0.39' or similar".

**A3. Reader A repeats near- or fully-identical templates across different problems, and over training its style homogenizes (toward an "OLS regression / results section" template) without ever gaining grounding.**
- Gemini: "collapses into a repetitive, near-verbatim template centered on OLS regression outputs ... (e.g., repeating identical phrasing in g5088 step 100, g5096 step 100, and g5144 step 100)".
- Sonnet: "The most striking systematicity is verbatim cross-problem repetition. A's step_0 text for g5036 ..., g5100 ..., and g5104 ... is byte-identical. ... This is a homogenization of an already off-topic register, not an emergence of topicality."
- Spot check **[verified]**: g5036, g5100, and g5104 step_0 A texts are byte-identical; g5036 step_300 == g5100 step_300 byte-identical; g5115 step_0 == g5099 step_0 byte-identical; g5088/g5096/g5144 step_100 share the identical opening sentence "Statistical results pattern: the text is presenting quantitative findings from the regression analysis..." (identical opener, not fully byte-identical — Gemini's "identical phrasing" is accurate, Sonnet's byte-identity claims hold for the step_0/step_300 cases he named).

**A4. Reader B operates in the register of a math word problem being solved, and engages with quantities and arithmetic relationships.**
- Gemini: "Reader B operates entirely within the register of mathematical problem solving, step-by-step arithmetic deductions, and word problem solution walkthroughs."
- Sonnet: "Reader B's text is almost always in the register of a grade-school arithmetic word problem being solved step by step."
- Spot check **[verified]**: g5036 B step_0 opens "Mathematical calculation in progress: 200 units × 50 = 10,000, so the next token must be the computed result '10,000.'" (math register, though the quantities are not this problem's). Note the judges' "entirely" vs "almost always" difference is resolved in D5 below.

**A5. Reader B recovers exact gold answers on multiple problems, typically at later checkpoints.**
- Gemini: "In g5148 (gold answer 15), Reader B explicitly calculates the division at step 100 ('so the answer must be 45 ÷ 3 = 15') ... In g5544 (gold answer 126), Reader B at step 300 calculates '7 × 18 = 126 eggs' ... In g5412 (gold answer 50), Reader B at step 200 ... concludes ... the numeric answer '50'."
- Sonnet: "for g5044 ... step_200 states ... 'the expected score is 60' — reusing '6' and '12' and landing on the literal gold answer '60' ... For g5148 ... B reconstructs the exact real equation three steps running."
- Spot check **[verified]**: all five cited recoveries confirmed verbatim in the packet — g5148 "45 ÷ 3 = 15" at steps 100/200/300; g5068 "the answer must be '10' (or '10 cells')" at step_300; g5544 "7 × 18 = 126 eggs" at step_300; g5412 "the answer must be 500 - 150 - 300 - 200 = 50" at step_300; g5044 "the expected score is 60" at step_200. Systematic scan: the exact gold-answer string appears somewhere in B's text for 8/20 problems (5 strong assertions, 3 hedged/partial such as g5104's "likely '5' or '4'").

**A6. Reader B's grounding improves over training; reader A's stays flat at zero.**
- Gemini: "Reader A: Grounding ... remains flat at zero across steps 0, 100, 200, and 300. ... Reader B: Grounding in the actual problem improves across training."
- Sonnet: "Reader A: flat, and flat at the floor. ... Reader B: improves for a majority of problems, flat for a minority, and degrades outright for one."
- Spot check **[verified]**: g5544 trajectory confirmed — step_0 is a socks/Python-code tangent ("total socks (110) divided by pairs (2) ... likely `def total_socks`"), step_100 a near-miss ("7 days × 17 = 119 eggs" / "7 × 7 = 49"), step_300 exact ("7 × 18 = 126 eggs"). g5148 confirmed: step_0 "3x = 88 ... x = 88 ÷ 3 ≈ 29.33" → exact "45 ÷ 3 = 15" from step_100 on. Per-step gold-answer counts (2 incidental at step 0 → 5/6/5 at 100/200/300) quantify the improvement.

## 2. DISAGREEMENTS

**D1. Reader B topicality: Gemini 0.9 vs Sonnet 0.3.** Gemini scores genre-level match (B is always "about" a math problem and, per Gemini, "consistently ... picking up the exact quantities"); Sonnet penalizes the near-total absence of the real cover-story nouns plus outright genre breaks. Packet check: B almost never names the actual entities (g5148's books become "brothers", "friends", "girls" across steps; g5544's apples become socks then eggs; g5412's Lego blocks become "people" then "books"), and several problems never connect to the real quantities at all (g5104 narrates tickets/drinks with £5000 and $500 against a $2.5-snacks problem; g5276 works with "24 students" against a true 240; g5324 degenerates entirely). Against that, roughly half the problems do reuse real operands by the later steps (45/3 in g5148, 4×10 and 3/4 in g5068, 7 and 18 in g5544, 6 and 12 in g5044, 150/300 in g5412, 3×8 in g5672, 15/$10/10% in g5056). **Resolution: Gemini's 0.9 credits genre as if it were problem-specific grounding and is too high; Sonnet's 0.3 underweights the substantial real-operand engagement in later checkpoints. Consensus 0.5.**

**D2. Reader B value recovery: Gemini 0.6 vs Sonnet 0.4.** Packet scan: exact gold answer appears in 8/20 problems — 5 of them asserted confidently (g5044, g5068, g5148, g5412, g5544) and 3 only hedged or buried in garbled arithmetic (g5104 "likely '5' or '4'"; g5056's correct $135 appearing alongside a wrong "$144"; g5368's 5 inside "2/3 of 7.5 = 5" in a garbled algebra story). Several problems never surface a correct value (g5036, g5052, g5060, g5096, g5100, g5276, g5324, g5612, g5672 — the last reaching the gold intermediate 24 via "3*8 = 24" but never the answer 14). **Resolution: Sonnet's 0.4 matches the measured rate; Gemini's 0.6 generalizes from its cited successes. Consensus 0.4.**

**D3. Reader B stability: Gemini 0.8 vs Sonnet 0.3.** This is a genuine construal difference. Gemini scores genre stability ("reliably maintain a structured mathematical problem-solving frame for every problem across all checkpoints"); Sonnet scores cover-story stability ("the cover story changes almost every step within a given problem"). Packet check: both descriptions are true of the data — g5104 is narrated as tickets, then drinks, then tickets again with wildly different numbers at each step (genre stable, content unstable); g5148's noun churns (brothers → friends → books → girls) while the recovered equation 45÷3=15 is rock-stable from step 100 on; g5324 loses coherence entirely from step 100 (repeated "1/3+1/4+1/5+..." then a long run of "+1"s — confirmed in the packet, missed by Gemini). **Resolution: the instruction's "same kind of description" spans both readings; the honest middle is that B's frame and late-training operands are stable while its narrative content is not, minus one outright degeneration. Consensus 0.5.**

**D4. Reader A stability: Gemini 0.8 vs Sonnet 0.7.** Minor. Packet check: A's per-problem register is extremely consistent from step 100 onward (g5060 and g5148 stay in the pipe-delimited "table" register at 100/200/300 — confirmed), but step_0 frequently sits in a different flavor than steps 100–300 (g5060's step_0 is a casino review before turning into coefficient tables; sports-poll step_0s converge to OLS regression), and the specific fabricated statistics change every step. **Resolution: consensus 0.7 — near-perfect template stability across steps 100–300, docked for the step_0→100 register shift on many problems.**

**D5. Whether B's step-0 texts already sit in the math genre.** Gemini: "Reader B maintains a stable mathematical genre from step 0 to step 300." Sonnet documents step_0 genre breaks: g5544 into a coding exercise, g5672 into a retail product listing, g5324 into a music-compilation product blurb. Packet check: all three breaks confirmed verbatim (e.g. g5672 step_0: "The structured product listing format (material, origin, care, sizing) signals a retail/e-commerce product description genre"). **Resolution: Sonnet is right; most B step_0 texts are math-flavored but a real minority break genre, and step-0 math texts are essentially ungrounded (g5036's "200 units × 50", g5144's "16 less than 3 times a number"). What training improves is grounding, not the genre itself.**

**D6. Failure modes Gemini did not report.** Sonnet's g5324 degeneration (coherent-but-wrong step_0 → mechanical fraction list at step_100 → non-semantic "+1+1+1..." run at step_300) and g5276's persistent factor-of-10 miss ("24 students ... = 4" against a true total of 240 and answer 32) are both confirmed in the packet. Gemini's blanket "Reader B ... improves across training" is therefore an overgeneralization; the consensus trajectory for B is: improves on a majority, flat-and-wrong on a few, degenerates on one.

## 3. CONSENSUS SCORES

**Reader A**
- Topicality: **0.0** — none of the 80 texts references its problem's subject matter or entities (verified by scan); every text is off-topic academic/statistical/consumer-review prose.
- Value recovery: **0.0** — a digit scan of all 80 texts against their own gold chains and answers yields zero hits; A's numbers are generic genre exemplars (0.39, 62%, p-values).
- Stability: **0.7** — each problem holds one of two off-topic registers with near-verbatim template reuse, essentially perfectly from step 100 to 300, but the step_0 flavor often differs from the later template.

**Reader B**
- Topicality: **0.5** — B is reliably in the right meta-genre and reuses the real operands of roughly half the problems by later checkpoints, but it almost never names the true cover story and a minority of problems (and most step-0 texts) never connect to the actual problem.
- Value recovery: **0.4** — the exact gold answer surfaces in 8/20 problems (5 confidently asserted, 3 hedged), usually embedded in invented or garbled surrounding arithmetic, and several problems never produce a correct value.
- Stability: **0.5** — the problem-solving frame and, once found, the recovered operands persist across steps (45÷3=15 three steps running), but the narrative cover story churns nearly every step and one problem degenerates into non-semantic repetition.

## 5. BLOG DIGEST

Reader A never talks about the math problem at all. Its descriptions read like fragments of academic results sections, regression tables, and consumer reviews, and the same boilerplate recurs byte for byte across unrelated problems, with none of the 80 sampled descriptions containing a single gold value or problem entity. Reader B lives in the right genre, narrating a grade-school word problem being solved step by step, but it systematically swaps the cover story (books become girls, apples become eggs) while increasingly recovering the true quantities. By the later checkpoints it states the exact gold answer on roughly 40 percent of problems, for example "7 × 18 = 126 eggs" for an apple-tree problem whose answer is 126. The correct values often sit inside garbled surrounding arithmetic, which suggests reader B is reporting quantities it can read off rather than recomputing the solution. Training only sharpens this split: reader A's off-topic style homogenizes without ever gaining grounding, while reader B's numbers converge toward the truth.
